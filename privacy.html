Photo Viewer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy covers Photo Viewer (Super-Fast) ("the app"), published by Utopia Softech Inc., including the Microsoft Store version and the version downloaded from GitHub.

Summary

The app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. It works entirely on your device.

Data the app processes

  • Your photos and their metadata (EXIF, GPS, XMP, IPTC). These are read from your device only so the app can show and edit them. They are never uploaded or sent anywhere.
  • App settings (window position and whether the details panel is open). These are stored locally in your Windows user registry and never leave your device.

Network access

The app makes no network connections of its own. It has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising or crash reporting.

If you click Open location in map for a photo that contains GPS coordinates, the app asks Windows to open your web browser at OpenStreetMap with those coordinates. That happens only when you choose to click it. From then on, OpenStreetMap's own privacy policy applies.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
